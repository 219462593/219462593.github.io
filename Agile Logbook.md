## Reflection

---
My logbook shows that the team moved from planning and slide preparation to delivering the SafeStreet presentation, but the same blockers appeared repeatedly: time pressure, difficulty coordinating meeting times, nervousness during presenting, and time management. These patterns suggest that our planning focused on tasks but did not surface dependencies and availability early enough. The walkthrough also showed that the team stayed aligned with the taskboard, but still needed clearer coordination. If I repeated this sprint, I would add a short dependency and availability check to planning, assign presentation roles earlier, and schedule a practice run sooner. Reflecting on the presentation feedback helped us identify improvements, but the next step is to turn that feedback into concrete actions for the next sprint. Overall, the logbook taught me that Agile works best when stand-ups, taskboards, and reflections are used together to expose problems and close the loop through action.
